KITRO · Sustainability Tech, Hospitality — Paid Social

Turning a hotel kitchen's food-waste data into a paid growth engine

KITRO's AI camera helps kitchens see exactly what they're throwing away. I translated that into a LinkedIn ad program built around a real client story: the Zurich Marriott Hotel cut food waste and avoidable cost within months of adopting the system. I paired quote-led creative from the Executive Chef with a data-driven case study format, and tested outcome-led messaging (up to 60% less waste) against proof-led creative built on the Marriott case study.

KITRO · Organic Social & Employer Branding

Making sustainability tangible, one post at a time

Alongside paid, I ran KITRO's organic LinkedIn presence — mixing employee-led storytelling for recruiting, plain-language sustainability education tied to SDG 12, and a cut-down of the Marriott success story built for both social and YouTube. The goal was to make a fairly technical B2B product feel human, whether the reader was a future hire or a hospitality operator.

Basel Area Business & Innovation · Organic Social, Public Sector

A full year of consistent organic growth, no paid spend

As a client of my own agency, Stula², I planned and ran a full 12-month LinkedIn content strategy for Basel Area — a public-sector innovation hub connecting startups, scale-ups, and established companies across life sciences, healthtech, and advanced manufacturing. The focus was steady, organic momentum over the course of a year rather than one-off spikes, building a consistent voice for a regional economic development brand. Over the 12-month period the account reached 138,501 impressions with 1,942 reactions, 39 comments, and 28 reposts — entirely organic.
