KITRO · Sustainability Tech, Hospitality — Paid Social · 2025

Turning a hotel kitchen's food-waste data into a paid growth engine

KITRO's AI camera helps kitchens see exactly what they're throwing away. I translated that into a LinkedIn ad program built around a real client story: the Zurich Marriott Hotel cut food waste and avoidable cost within months of adopting the system. I paired quote-led creative from the Executive Chef with a data-driven case study format, and tested outcome-led messaging (up to 60% less waste) against proof-led creative built on the Marriott case study.

Making sustainability tangible, one post at a time

Alongside paid, I ran KITRO's organic LinkedIn presence — mixing employee-led storytelling for recruiting, plain-language sustainability education tied to SDG 12, and a cut-down of the Marriott success story built for both social and YouTube.
